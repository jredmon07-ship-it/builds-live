    Halden Tide

    REF. TD-40-BK

    The Tide is the house's dive watch. Cream luminous markers sit on a black dial under a flat sapphire, the steel bezel turns one way only, and the case is water resistant to 200 metres. [SAMPLE] At 40 mm on its steel bracelet, it is the largest watch we make.
